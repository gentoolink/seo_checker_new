Why Your Business Isn't Showing on Google — And What You Can Do About It

If you've ever typed your business name into Google… and nothing came up, you're not alone. It's frustrating. You've done the work. You've set up your website. Maybe even claimed your Google Business Profile. But for some reason, you're still invisible when it matters most.

This post is for small business owners, tradespeople, and local pros who are wondering:

"Why isn't my business showing up when people search for me?"

Let's walk through the most common reasons — and how to fix them, fast.

🔍 1. You Haven't Claimed Your Google Business Profile (GBP)

This is step one. If you haven't officially claimed your GBP listing, you're at the mercy of public suggestions and whatever Google thinks your info might be.

How to fix it:

  • Go to google.com/business
  • Click "Manage now"
  • Search your business name and follow the steps

It's free, and it gives you control over how your business appears on Google Maps and Search.

⭐ 2. You Don't Have Enough Reviews

This is one of the most overlooked ranking signals. A business with 0–2 reviews is going to get buried under others with 10–20+ reviews.

And yes — it's absolutely possible to start asking for reviews without feeling pushy. One quick, polite message like this works:

"Thanks again for choosing us! If you had a great experience, here's a link to leave a quick Google review. It helps a lot!" 💬

Want your direct review link? Use our free generator here.

📸 3. Your Profile Looks Empty

Google likes active businesses. If your profile has:

  • No photos
  • No updates
  • No hours
  • No description…

…it's probably not going to rank well. You've got to look "alive."

Add these today:

  • 3–5 real photos (outside, inside, product/service)
  • An updated business description
  • A simple post about a promotion or service you offer

📍 4. You're Competing in a Crowded Market

If you're a plumber in Vancouver or a realtor in Toronto… there's a lot of competition.

That means you need to go a little further:

  • Add location-based keywords in your business description (e.g. "Emergency plumbing in Burnaby")
  • Ask every happy customer for a review
  • Post updates weekly — even if it's just a quick service tip or reminder
