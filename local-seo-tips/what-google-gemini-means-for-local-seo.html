🤖 What Google Gemini Means for Local SEO

You've probably heard the buzz around Google Gemini (formerly Bard), but here's what matters: it's not just an AI chatbot — it's changing how Google search works.

Gemini powers Google's new Search Generative Experience (SGE), which means when someone searches for a local service (like "plumber in Maple Ridge"), they might see a full AI-generated summary before they ever see a list of websites.

That means:

  • Your site could be referenced in the answer… without getting clicked
  • Google is relying more on structured, trustworthy content to fuel those summaries
  • If your business doesn't show up in those summaries? You're invisible

This is a big shift.

But it's not a reason to panic — it's a reason to get smart about your content.

What You Can Do

If you want Gemini (and Google) to notice your business, you need to:

  • Add FAQ-style content to your site — short, clear answers
  • Use real location keywords, like the cities or neighborhoods you serve
  • Keep your Google Business Profile updated and active
  • Earn reviews and respond to them — credibility counts
  • Use structured data (schema) where you can

Gemini may summarize search results, but it still needs real-world businesses to pull from. If your content is helpful and well-organized, you have a shot at being the business it showcases.
